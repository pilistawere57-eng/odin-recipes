My Chicken Salad

Description

Chicken salad is a delicious and healthy dish made with cooked chicken, fresh vegetables, and a creamy dressing.

It is easy to prepare and can be served for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken
  • 1 cup chopped lettuce
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 cucumber
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the chicken and allow it to cool.
  2. Cut the chicken into small pieces.
  3. Wash and chop the vegetables.
  4. Place all ingredients in a large bowl.
  5. Add mayonnaise, salt, and pepper.
  6. Mix everything together.
  7. Serve and enjoy your chicken salad.
